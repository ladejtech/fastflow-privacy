Delete your FastFlow data

FastFlow: Fasting & Water, by Ladej

FastFlow has no user accounts and no servers. We never receive your fasting, water or weight data, so there is nothing stored with us to delete. Your data lives only on your phone and, if you turned on sync, in a private folder in your own Google Drive. Here is how to delete each copy.

1. Data on your phone

Uninstall FastFlow, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → FastFlow → Storage → Clear storage. This permanently deletes all fasts, water logs, weight entries, notes and settings on that phone straight away.

2. Your Google Drive copy (only if you used "Sign in with Google")

In the app: Settings → Delete cloud data, then Sign out. The copy is removed from your Drive immediately.

Without the app: open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → FastFlow → Options → Delete hidden app data. You can also remove FastFlow's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions. This data is held by Google, not by us. To limit or remove it, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). In regions that require consent, you can also change your choice in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google explains how long it keeps this data at policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

FastFlow Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

FastFlow ("the app") is a free intermittent fasting and hydration tracker, supported by ads.

What stays on your phone

Your fasts, water logs, weight entries, notes, moods and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers and we never receive this data.

Optional Google sign-in and Drive sync

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app receives your Google account email address and permission to use a private app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). Your data is saved there so you can restore it on a new phone. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files, and we cannot see this folder. You can sign out at any time, or use "Delete cloud data" in Settings to remove the Drive copy.

Backup files

If you create a backup file, it is saved or shared only where you choose.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about how ads are viewed. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your health or fasting data for advertising.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your phone. Use "Delete cloud data" in Settings to remove your Google Drive copy.

Health information

FastFlow is for general wellness and is not medical advice.

Changes

We will update this policy in the app if anything changes.
